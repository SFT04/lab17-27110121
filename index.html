We spilled our 9-to-5s.

In 2026, a few bakery and coffee lovers looked up from their spreadsheets and asked one question: what if every morning smelled like butter and fresh espresso? So we swapped boardrooms for ovens and deadlines for dough. Spill. is the result: a cosy corner of Lahore where the croissants are flaky, the coffee is strong, and the tea is always worth spilling.
